About InterviewAI Pro

Our Mission

To democratize access to high-quality interview preparation through advanced AI technology, helping candidates land their dream roles with confidence.

How It Works

InterviewAI Pro leverages Google's Gemini AI to simulate realistic interview environments. By parsing your resume and desired role, it generates contextual questions and evaluates your spoken or written responses in real-time.

Technology

Built with Python, Flask, and Bootstrap 5, powered by Google Gemini AI for natural language processing and Web Speech API for voice recognition.
